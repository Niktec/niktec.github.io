Privacy Policy

Effective as of 2026-10-03

📄 Introduction

This policy applies to the DNS Speed Test app (the "Application") for Android, iOS, Windows and macOS, created by Niktec ("we", "us") as a free service.

📊 What Stays on Your Device

There are no accounts. Your DNS server list, test results and settings are stored only on your device. Exports and imports are files you create or pick yourself; the Application never uploads them.

The Application does not collect your precise location. The services listed below may derive an approximate location (country or region) from your IP address.

On Android, your settings may be included in your Google device backup if you have backup turned on.

📡 What the Application Sends

  • DNS tests: the servers you test receive your DNS queries, including your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Server list: at startup the Application downloads the default server list and ad settings from our Cloudflare Workers service. Cloudflare sees your IP address when it serves them.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports: they contain a random ID, your system, language and app version, but no DNS servers or results. Statistics count app starts and speed tests. See Your Choices below.

🔗 Third Party Services

These services may collect data such as your IP address, device and app information, and, for ads, your advertising ID. Their own privacy policies apply.

Advertising and consent

  • Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform (Android, iOS)

Analytics

  • Google Analytics for Firebase (Android, iOS)
  • Google Analytics 4 (Windows, macOS)

Crash reporting

  • Firebase Crashlytics (Android, iOS)
  • Sentry (Windows, macOS)

Purchases

  • Google Play Billing (Android)
  • RevenueCat (Android)

Other

  • Google Play Services and In-App Review (Android)
  • Cloudflare (server list) (all platforms)

Apart from the services above, we share information with others only if required by law.

🚪 Your Choices

Android and iOS: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the Application asks for your consent to personalized ads. If you decline, ads are shown without personalization or as limited ads, which do not use advertising identifiers, and usage statistics and crash reports are turned off. In US states with privacy laws, the Application shows a choice to opt out of the sharing of your data for personalized ads. You can change your choice any time under Settings → Privacy & Ads. On iOS you can also deny tracking when asked.

The Application does not respond to Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signals. Use the in-app settings above instead.

Desktop: the Application asks on first launch whether it may send usage statistics and crash reports. If you decline, both are off. Change them any time under Settings → Privacy.

Uninstalling the Application removes its data from your device (on desktop, also delete the DnsSpeedTest folder in your app data directory).

⚖ Legal Bases (GDPR)

  • Ads on Android and iOS: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG) via Google's consent message where required. You can withdraw it at any time; this does not affect earlier processing. Limited ads, and ads where no consent is required, rely on our legitimate interest in financing the free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Usage statistics and crash reports: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG) wherever a consent dialog is shown, which is on desktop and on Android and iOS where required; otherwise our legitimate interest in running and improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings.
  • Purchases (Android): performing the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Server list and DNS tests: providing the app's function (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), and our legitimate interest in a working app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

🌐 International Transfers

Google, Cloudflare, Sentry and RevenueCat may process data in the United States and other countries outside the EEA. Where required, this relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

🗃 Data Retention

  • On your device: until you delete it or uninstall the Application.
  • Our servers: none. We store no user data ourselves.
  • Google Analytics: deleted automatically after 14 months.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: 90 days. Sentry: 30 days.
  • RevenueCat and Google: according to their own policies and legal retention duties.

🔑 Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete and restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority. Providing data is not required to use the Application, and there is no automated decision-making.

Because the Application has no accounts, we usually cannot link data held by the services above to you.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law. Contact us at the address below. In Germany, the competent supervisory authority is the LDI NRW (Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen).

🇺🇸 US State Privacy Rights

If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law, you may have the right to know what personal data we process, to delete or correct it, and to opt out of the sharing of personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell personal data for money. Ads on Android and iOS may involve sharing your advertising ID and device information with Google. You can opt out in Settings → Privacy & Ads. We do not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. To make a request, contact us at the address below.

👶 Children

The Application is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
